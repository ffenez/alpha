Local modification (2026-08-09): the upstream font lacks cmap entries for
Cyrillic capital O (U+041E) and Pe (U+041F). Two cmap mappings were added:
U+041E -> existing glyph "O" (Latin capital O, identical shape) and
U+041F -> existing glyph "Pi" (Greek capital Pi, identical shape).
No glyph outlines were changed. Patch applied with fontTools.
